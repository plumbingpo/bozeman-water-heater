Kitchen Hot Water

Restaurant and Food Service Water Heaters

Dish machines, sanitizing sinks, and handwashing stations all compete for the same hot water, and a heater that's undersized or failing reveals itself as a dish machine that can't hit temperature and stacks of dirty plates. A high-temperature dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater paired with the main system. We handle high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Hot water temperature is a critical issue for any food-service operator. Health inspectors check water temperature at handwashing sinks and sanitizing stations, and a dish machine that can't reach its rinse temperature puts the whole operation at risk. Our work keeps your system delivering the temperatures and volume your operation depends on, with documented service records that demonstrate a well-maintained facility to health inspectors and landlords. We provide detailed reports after each visit, along with the documentation many commercial leases require.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians are used to working in occupied facilities, installing after close when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping part of the system online where possible.

Hotels & Multifamily

Hotel, Multifamily, and Large-Facility Water Heaters

Bigger buildings bring bigger stakes. One failed tank in a hotel can leave an entire floor with lukewarm showers, and an apartment building with a single central heater needs a real plan, not random service calls. Bozeman Water Heater provides the experience and structure these systems demand, from recirculation pumps and mixing valves to multi-unit installations.

For a large facility, downtime is measured in refunds and tenant complaints, so we plan for prevention first. That means full system assessments, sizing based on real occupancy, multiple heaters piped in parallel so one failed unit never leaves the building cold, recirculation service, and routine flushing and descaling to hold capacity. When something does fail, our repair team isolates it so the rest of the system keeps running.

Safety

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

Safety is part of every commercial job we do. Our technicians inspect flue draft and vent connections, verify proper combustion air, check relief valves and their discharge lines, confirm expansion tanks are charged correctly, and set mixing valves so water can be stored hot enough to control bacteria while reaching the tap at a safe temperature. Permits and inspections are handled as part of the job. You receive a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test results, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and expose the owner to tenant disputes. Bozeman Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Bozeman and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Our technicians are equipped to handle any commercial water heater emergency, including failed gas valves, burned-out heating elements, ignition and flame sensor faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, failed recirculation pumps, and tankless units stuck in lockout. We prioritize diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water as quickly as possible, valving off a failed unit so the rest of the system keeps running, then put maintenance in place to keep it from happening again. Every commercial response includes thorough documentation for your service records.

Maintenance Programs

Scheduled Maintenance for Commercial Hot Water

Our commercial maintenance program is built around your equipment, how the building uses hot water, and your budget. On each scheduled visit, we handle anode checks and tank flushing, descaling of tankless heat exchangers, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and gas valve inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve service, and temperature verification at the tap. Hard water cuts the life of commercial equipment quickly, so high-use properties usually benefit from quarterly visits.

Scheduled maintenance typically costs a fraction of an emergency replacement and sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Properties on a program usually see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and steadier water temperatures. Call us to discuss a plan designed for your property's needs and budget.
