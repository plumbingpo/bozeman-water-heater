What makes a water heater installation different from a replacement? Mostly, the new work. Adding a heater for an addition, relocating one out of a living space, upgrading to a larger tank or a tankless unit, or converting between gas, electric, and heat pump all require piping, venting, or wiring that did not exist before. Bozeman Water Heater plans the whole job up front so the heater performs, the installation passes, and nothing gets improvised on the day.

Before any pipe is cut, we match the heater to your household. Tanks are compared by first-hour rating, not just gallons, and tankless units are sized by gallons per minute at the temperature rise your water needs in winter. Next, we choose the spot together. Gas models require a vent route outdoors and adequate combustion air. A heat pump model has to have breathing room, temperatures above roughly 40 degrees, and a condensate drain.   • A tank that cannot keep up with your household anymore
  • New construction or a major renovation that needs a heater from scratch
  • Interest in tankless or heat pump efficiency
  • A new bathroom, addition, or finished basement that needs more hot water
  • A large soaking tub that a standard tank cannot fill
  • Long waits for hot water at faucets far from the heater

The Benefits of Professional Water Heater Installation

Installing a water heater from scratch involves gas, venting, electrical, and plumbing work all at once. Gas lines need sizing for the whole system's load, plus a leak test. A vent outside the manufacturer's limits on length, diameter, slope, or clearance can put carbon monoxide into living space. Electric, tankless, and heat pump heaters need circuits matched to their load, which can exceed what an older panel offers. The plumbing side needs a correctly rated relief valve and a full-size discharge line, and depending on location, code may require an expansion tank, a drain pan, seismic straps, or a permit and inspection.

Professional service handles every trade involved, not just setting the heater. Sizing by first-hour rating or flow rate, tested gas piping, a vent routed to spec, a circuit matched to the load, and the expansion tank, pan, or straps your code requires are all included. Gas units get a draft and carbon monoxide check before we leave. You end up with an installation that is safe, properly sized, and ready for inspection. Simple Ways to Extend Your Water Heater's Life

A few preventive steps can help you avoid a cold shower or a wet floor down the road. Follow these tips from our technicians:

  • Keep a clear path to the heater so it can be serviced and inspected easily
  • Store paint, gasoline, and solvents far from any gas water heater
  • Descale tankless units once a year, or more often with hard water
  • Check the drain pan and relief valve discharge line now and then for signs of water
  • Drain a few gallons from a new tank twice a year to control sediment
  • Schedule regular maintenance, including an anode rod check
  • A 120-degree setting protects against burns and slows mineral buildup
